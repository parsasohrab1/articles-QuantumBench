# Business Requirements Specification – QuantumBench

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Compare the energy obtained by VQE with classical Hartree–Fock and exact (FCI) energies on the same molecule. |
| BR-2 | Quantify the measurement (shot) cost needed to report an energy within chemical accuracy (1.6 mHa). |
| BR-3 | Report classical wall-clock time next to the quantum shot budget. |
| BR-4 | State that no hardware noise is simulated and that no advantage is claimed. |

Out of scope: hardware execution, noise models, larger molecules, error mitigation.
